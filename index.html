Roadie Support

Roadie helps a learner driver and their supervising adult log supervised driving hours toward a license.

Need help or found a bug?
Email a@mllr.cc and I'll get back to you. In the app, you can also tap Settings → Send feedback or report a bug, which pre-fills your app version and device to make issues easier to fix.

Getting started

  • Add your car under Vehicles. Set its Bluetooth so Roadie can stop a drive automatically when your phone disconnects from the car.
  • Start a drive from the phone, from CarPlay, or from your Apple Watch. Roadie records your route, distance, and time until you stop or disconnect.
  • Set your goal under Settings to track progress toward your state's supervised-driving requirement.

Hands-free start

  • CarPlay: turn on "Start a drive automatically" for your CarPlay car under Vehicles.
  • Bluetooth-only car: follow Settings → Hands-free start to set up a one-time Shortcuts automation that starts a drive when you connect.

Common questions

  • Does a drive keep recording if my phone locks? Yes. Roadie keeps measuring your route while a drive is in progress, even with the phone locked.
  • What if I forget to stop a drive? Roadie stops automatically when you disconnect from the car, and it also stops a drive left stationary for a while, trimming the parked time so your hours stay accurate.
  • Is my data private? Yes. Your drives stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
